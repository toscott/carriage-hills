A Reminder About Our Covenants & Communication With the Board

The Board would like to provide all homeowners with a general reminder about several provisions of our Declaration of Covenants and Restrictions, as well as information about communicating with the Association.

Trailers and Similar Vehicles

Our governing documents contain restrictions regarding the storage or parking of trailers on residential lots. Homeowners should be mindful of these restrictions when storing trailers or similar equipment on their property.

We also recognize that homeowners may occasionally have a temporary need for a trailer, moving vehicle, construction equipment, or similar item. For example, during a move, home renovation, or other temporary circumstance.

If you have a temporary situation that may raise a question under the covenants, please contact the Board and let us know the circumstances and expected duration. This allows the Board to understand the situation before determining whether further action is necessary.

Swimming Pools

The Board is also aware that the Declaration contains restrictions relating to swimming pools.

The Board's current practice is to distinguish between permanent above-ground pool installations and temporary/seasonal pools that are intended to be removed at the end of the swimming season.

If you have questions about whether a particular pool installation is permitted, please contact the Board before beginning installation.

How to Report a Concern or Contact the Board

Homeowners who have questions or concerns regarding the covenants are encouraged to contact the Association directly. Please use the Association's main email address when possible so that communications can be maintained as part of the Association's records and remain accessible to future Boards.

Please provide when reporting a potential covenant concern:

  • The property address involved, if applicable;
  • A description of the concern;
  • Any relevant dates or circumstances; and
  • Photographs or other information that may help the Board understand the situation, when available.

The Board does not routinely patrol the subdivision for covenant violations. Concerns brought to the Board will be reviewed based on the governing documents, the circumstances involved, the information available to the Board, and the Association's available resources.

The Board's goal is to encourage voluntary compliance whenever reasonably possible. Formal enforcement, including legal action, may be considered when the Board determines that it is appropriate. Please allow the Board time to review reported concerns. The Board may not be able to provide details regarding actions involving another homeowner or property.

Please Do Not Confront Other Homeowners on Behalf of the Association

Homeowners are encouraged to bring concerns directly to the Board rather than confronting another homeowner about an alleged covenant violation on behalf of the Association. This helps the Board remain neutral and allows concerns to be handled consistently and without unnecessary conflict between neighbors.

Looking Ahead

Our Declaration was established many years ago, and the Board recognizes that the needs and expectations of our community may change over time.

As part of our ongoing efforts to understand what homeowners want from the Association, we would like to hear from residents about which portions of the current covenants they believe are important to retain, should be clarified or updated, or may no longer reflect the community's needs.

More information regarding opportunities for homeowner feedback and potential future amendments will be provided as the Board discusses these matters.

Interested in Helping?

The Association is operated by volunteers. Homeowners who are interested in serving on the Board or assisting the Association are encouraged to contact us. Our goal is to maintain a functional Association while treating homeowners fairly, consistently, and respectfully.

Thank you to everyone who contributes their time and effort to our community.
